# Grove - Button v1.0

Functional and mechanical tscircuit recreation of Seeed Studio SKU 101020003.
The signal is pulled low by 10 kΩ and driven to VCC while the button is held.

## Source fidelity

- Revision: v1.0, 20 mm × 20 mm.
- Source: [official Seeed Eagle archive](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Grove_Button/resources/Grove_-_Button_v1.0_Source_File.zip) and [product wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Button/).
- Modeled: Grove connector pinout, button, pull-down, board size, mounting holes, and representative placement/routing.
- The published Eagle files use a legacy binary format, so copper and silkscreen are recreated rather than converted exactly.
- License: the downloaded hardware archive contains no explicit license file; Seeed attribution is retained and redistribution rights should be confirmed independently.
