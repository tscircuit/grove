# Grove - Relay v1.2

Functional recreation of Seeed Studio SKU 103020005, including the regulated 3 V relay rail, S8050 low-side driver, flyback diode, indicator LED, and NO/COM/NC terminal.

## Source fidelity

- Revision: v1.2, 40 mm × 20 mm class Grove 2U outline.
- Source: [official Seeed Eagle archive](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/Grove-Relay/res/Grove-Relay_Eagle_Files.zip) and [product wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/Grove-Relay/).
- Values and net connectivity follow the Eagle schematic. Footprints, placement, outline notches, and copper are representative rather than a direct CAD conversion.
- License: the downloaded hardware archive contains no explicit license file; Seeed attribution is retained and redistribution rights should be confirmed independently.
